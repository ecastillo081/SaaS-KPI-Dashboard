Risks

  • Mean NRR of {{MEAN_NRR}} means the existing base shrinks unless new logos or reactivation replace leakage.
  • August NRR of {{AUG_NRR}} shows how quickly the beginning base can compress when churn spikes. Slower new-logo contribution then leaves less offset at the company level.
  • A model dependent on acquisition must continually replace recurring-revenue leakage. That is a less durable plan than expansion-led growth.

Opportunities

  • A real expansion / upsell motion would be the cleanest way to lift NRR above GRR, if product and customer behavior support it.
  • Finance should segment churn by channel, customer segment, cohort, and tenure before increasing acquisition spend.
  • Reactivation helped, but it is not a substitute for retaining the beginning base or building expansion.
Interpretation: These are analytical recommendations for how Finance should review the book, not actions that were implemented. Signup-cohort dollar retention can exceed 100% here because some subscriptions start after signup month and some logos later reactivate; it is not NRR and is not shown as a heatmap for that reason.

Management actions for Finance to challenge

  1. Separate the forecast into beginning-base retention, new-logo acquisition, reactivation, and expansion so growth is not treated as a single rate.
  2. Investigate churn concentration by segment, channel, and tenure before increasing acquisition spend.
  3. Establish an explicit expansion / upsell driver rather than relying on new logos to replace leakage.
  4. Use CAC payback thresholds that update as ARPU and retention change; do not freeze an 11-month average as a permanent hurdle.

What this book is telling Finance

  • Headline ARR growth through mid-2025 masked a missing expansion engine.
  • Falling ARPU lengthens effective payback even when monthly CAC is not rising.
  • Paid acquisition is the largest logo source and has the highest payback proxy in this mix.

Limitations

  • Synthetic data; results do not describe a real company.
  • Frozen window through {{AS_OF_DATE}}.
  • Assigned customer CAC, not GL marketing-spend allocation.
  • No true upgrade/downgrade activity in the fixture.
  • No causal inference and no production deployment.
